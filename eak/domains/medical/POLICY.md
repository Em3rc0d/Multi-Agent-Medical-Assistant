# Medical Domain Boundary

This pack is for research and professional decision support. It does not define autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, treatment selection, or unsupervised clinical action.

High-risk claims and imaging analyses require explicit review according to deployment policy. Patient or health-related artifacts are treated as restricted by default unless a deployment policy proves otherwise. External search or model egress must be allowed explicitly by the policy engine before provider invocation.

The original medical repository remains Quarry #001. Its existing model outputs are not promoted to production certification merely by being wrapped as EAK providers.
